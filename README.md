## Problem

Linear algebra is a required first-year course for engineering students. Beginners may struggle to visualize linear transformations and understand what a matrix does to points in 3D. However, students with aphantasia are unable to visualize things in their head making their linear algebra learning even harder. Being unable to build these mental pictures, students are unable to excel in MATH 152 and succeed in their future engineering studies. A linear transformation encoded as a matrix can be manually visualized through a lecture visualize or YouYube video, but these are one-off solutions unable to adapt to a specific matrix. ([source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1615860))
